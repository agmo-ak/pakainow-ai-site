About Ling

Founder of PakaiNow.AI | FashionTech Visionary | Designer Turned Problem Solver

🌸 From Art to Innovation

Ling’s story begins with a love for art — the kind that blends beauty, meaning, and human connection.
After graduating with a Bachelor of Arts, she started her career in design, not just to make things look good, but to make them feel right.

Over the next 15 years, she built her career in the tech world, eventually leading creative teams as Head of Design in a public-listed technology firm.
Her focus was always on one thing: understanding people.
How they see.
How they click.
How they decide.

That curiosity — to understand the human side of technology — became the foundation of everything she would later build.

👗 A Shopper’s Frustration Turned Into a FashionTech Revolution

Outside of work, Ling had another passion: online fashion shopping.
Her wardrobe told stories from every corner of the internet — but her return pile told another.

“At least one parcel a week went back. Wrong fit. Wrong cut. Looked great online, but not on me.”

That experience, repeated again and again, sparked an idea:
What if technology could bridge the gap between imagination and reality?
What if shoppers could see themselves wearing a product before buying it?

It wasn’t just a designer’s curiosity anymore — it became a mission.

💡 The Birth of PakaiNow.AI

From that everyday frustration came PakaiNow.AI — a name that means “Wear Now” in Bahasa Malaysia.
The idea was simple yet revolutionary:

To build an AI-powered platform where anyone can upload a photo, select an outfit, and instantly see themselves wearing it — in motion, in context, in confidence.

No more guesswork. No more returns.
Just pure digital fashion magic, built right here in Malaysia.

Under Ling’s leadership, PakaiNow.AI has become ASEAN’s first Generative AI FashionTech platform, combining design, AI, and storytelling to transform how people shop and how brands connect with their customers.

🧠 Design Thinking Meets Human Empathy

Ling believes great technology doesn’t start with code — it starts with empathy.
Her years in UI/UX taught her to design not just for screens, but for feelings.

That philosophy lives in every pixel of PakaiNow.AI:

  • Intuitive interfaces that make AI feel human.
  • Personalized experiences that make fashion feel meaningful.
  • Ethical technology that protects user trust and creativity.

Her vision isn’t just to build an app — but to create an ecosystem where fashion, technology, and identity merge seamlessly.

🌏 Beyond Fashion — A Vision for ASEAN Innovation

As a Malaysian woman entrepreneur in tech, Ling’s journey is also about proving that world-class innovation can come from Southeast Asia — powered by local creativity and sovereign AI infrastructure.

She envisions PakaiNow.AI not only as a product, but as a movement — one that champions responsible AI, celebrates individuality, and inspires the next generation of women innovators.

💬 Ling’s Philosophy

“Technology should make people feel seen — not sold to.
When design meets empathy, and AI meets imagination, we don’t just change how people shop — we change how they express themselves.”
