🧠 About PakaiNow.AI

PakaiNow.AI is ASEAN’s first AI-powered virtual try-on platform that allows users to upload their photo, choose a t-shirt design, and instantly generate a short video of themselves wearing it — in different real-life scenes.

Built on sovereign AI cloud infrastructure, our technology merges personalization, virtual fashion, and e-commerce into one seamless experience.

We serve both consumers (B2C) — through our customizable t-shirt platform — and brands (B2B) through white-label AI try-on integrations for online and offline retail.

Pakai means “wear” in Bahasa Malaysia — and PakaiNow.AI means “wear now” — reflecting our mission to make fashion instantly accessible, expressive, and immersive.
