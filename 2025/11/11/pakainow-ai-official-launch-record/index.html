PakaiNow.AI – Official Launch Record

2025-11-11

PakaiNow.AI officially launched on 11 November 2025 as ASEAN’s first home-grown Generative AI-powered virtual try-on FashionTech platform, enabling users to upload a selfie and instantly generate AI-powered apparel try-on videos in real-world scenes.

This post serves as a permanent, timestamped public record of the platform’s launch for verification purposes, accreditation submissions, and external audits.

What Was Launched

On this date, PakaiNow.AI released its first production-ready version featuring:

  • AI-powered virtual try-on video generation
  • Selfie ingestion and identity-preserving body mapping
  • Dynamic scene library (pickleball court, office, dating, outdoor, etc.)
  • Customizable apparel templates
  • Personalized text/name printing
  • E-commerce pathway for real T-shirt purchase
  • Shopify-ready integration prototype

This marks the official introduction of the platform to the Malaysian and ASEAN market.

Significance of the Launch

  • First virtual try-on FashionTech platform fully developed in ASEAN
  • Built on a sovereign AI cloud with data residency in Malaysia
  • Made in Malaysia and recognized under the ASEAN Records category: “First Home-grown Generative AI-Powered Virtual Try-On FashionTech Platform”
  • Establishes Malaysia as a regional innovator in FashionTech & RetailTech

Version Released

Version: v1.0.0
Release Type: Public Beta
Deployment: Production server (Malaysia-based data center)
Main Capabilities: AI video try-on pipeline + front-end flow
