Enterprise Claude AI, Operated by Engineers Who Actually Build With It

Pakainow.ai runs, advises on, and implements Claude AI for organisations that need it to work in production — not just in a demo. We are a registered partner in the Claude Partner Network, and every engagement is led by our in-house engineering team.

Most companies don’t fail at AI because the technology is weak — they fail because nobody owns the operational side. Pakainow.ai exists to close that gap: we operate your Claude environment, advise on where it creates the most value, build the systems that put it to work, and — where needed — embed engineers directly inside your team to ship outcomes fast.

Deploying Claude is one thing. Keeping it tuned, secure, and cost-efficient month after month is another. Our managed service takes that operational load off your internal team entirely. It starts with full environment setup — configuration of models, access permissions, integrations, and workflow-specific context tuning from day one. From there, we continuously monitor and adjust the deployment so it stays efficient as your usage patterns evolve. Everything is invoiced locally in MYR, SST-compliant, with no withholding tax complications on cross-border service payments, and backed by a Malaysia-based support team you can actually reach in your own time zone. All operational changes go through our internal engineering governance process, so nothing changes in your environment without oversight, and your data is never used to train external models. It’s one straightforward service fee — no bundled markups, no hidden line items.

We don’t open a consulting engagement by describing what Claude can do. We open by mapping where your business is losing time, money, or accuracy — then work backwards to what Claude, agents, MCP, and structured automation can realistically fix. Our approach centres on a working session we call the Opportunity Mapping Workshop — a structured exercise that surfaces real operational bottlenecks, ranks them by impact and feasibility, identifies the systems and stakeholders involved, and defines what “done” looks like before a single line of code is written. You leave with a prioritised, business-cased AI roadmap — not a slide deck full of buzzwords. We typically advise on document-heavy and knowledge-intensive workflows suited to large-context processing, multi-step task automation using Claude agents, secure system connectivity via Model Context Protocol (MCP), developer productivity uplift using Claude Code, and reliable system integration via structured tool-based output.

Turning Claude into something reliable, observable, and cost-sensible at scale takes more than an API key. Our implementation team builds the underlying architecture properly the first time. That includes multi-agent orchestration, where multiple Claude agents coordinate with clear role separation, permissioning, and fallback handling; custom MCP servers that securely connect Claude to your internal databases, tools, and APIs; context and prompt architecture — structured context files, system prompts, and domain knowledge packs tailored to how your organisation actually operates; batch processing for cost control on high-volume workloads, cutting per-task inference cost significantly; developer workflow integration, embedding Claude Code into your engineering workflow for faster builds, reviews, and documentation; CI/CD-embedded automation, with automated code review summaries, release notes, and test coverage checks inside your existing pipeline; structured output pipelines that return clean, typed JSON for dependable downstream integration instead of fragile text parsing; retrieval architecture (RAG) — production retrieval systems over your document base, including chunking strategy, re-ranking, and citation-aware responses; and automated data pipelines that turn raw inputs like PDFs, spreadsheets, and emails into structured data your reporting and BI tools can use. Every build comes with clean, documented code built for internal handover, plus post-launch support and iteration.

Skip the hiring cycle. Our Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) work inside your team, on your actual systems and data, shipping real outcomes instead of proposals. We run on two-week delivery cycles, where every sprint ends with a working demo, not a status update — you’ll see tangible output within the first month. Our engineers are genuinely embedded: they join your standups, work inside your repositories and tools, and transfer knowledge as they build, so the capability stays with your team after the engagement ends. Delivery covers the full Claude stack, from context and prompt design through Agent SDK orchestration, MCP integration, retrieval pipelines, and structured output, end to end. Our FDEs use Claude Code as a daily tool, moving from concept to working prototype in days, then hardening it for production. Where appropriate, we also deploy autonomous workflow automation that monitors, decides, and acts across your systems, with guardrails built in from the start. This is a dedicated delivery cell of engineers and a business analyst, embedded directly in your organisation — reach out to discuss scope, team composition, and engagement structure for your specific use case.
